EDTECH / LEARNING SUPPORT01 — 02

GapMap

Know exactly what you don't understand.

GapMap is a learning platform designed for South African learners in Grades 10–12. It uses short diagnostic assessments to identify subject knowledge gaps, then turns those insights into a personalised learning path with targeted explanations, practice and learning resources.

Diagnostic assessments Visual knowledge-gap map Targeted learning path
Explore GapMap
FINTECH / FINANCIAL INCLUSION02 — 02

SmartMoney

Your money. Your plan. Your confidence.

SmartMoney is a free, offline-first money companion created for South Africans with irregular income and cash-based money habits. It helps people record income, plan essentials, build savings in small steps, manage group savings and recognise suspicious messages before they become costly scams.

Flexible money planning Personal and group savings Built-in scam awareness
